A NOTE TO PARENTS & EDUCATORS

Curiosity with guardrails.

The Shadowglitch Crew is a fictional educational adventure. It teaches children how to think critically about online information, look after their privacy, and practise technology skills responsibly. Always get permission before examining or testing a system. The free Mission 003, 009 and 011 games use only our own purpose-built, fictional training experiences. No accounts, email sign-up or personal submissions are needed.
